\section*{Introduction}

The project is the French Robotics Cup 2027; the goal is to make a robot to participate in the competition.
The project was proposed by Robotek Orléans.
This first report concentrates on the start of the project and project management.

\section{French Robotics Cup 2027}

The French Robotics Cup (CFR) is an annual competition that takes place in May. For 2027, the competition starts on May 5th and ends on May 9th. The competition is organized by Eurobot. Each year, the competition has a different theme; this year's theme is Kaamelott, and the different tasks are inspired by it. The competition consists of matches between two different teams, with a match duration of 100 seconds. The duel takes place in an area designed by Eurobot, which is available in the first appendix. To participate in the competition, the robot needs to pass the homologation phase. The goal of this phase is to ensure that the robot is not dangerous for the team or the opponents. The rulebook gives a different number of points depending on the tasks completed by the robot.

This year, the first task is to make a castle with towers and walls to protect the Grail. The second task is to attack the opponent's castle with the balls provided by the organizers. For more details, please see the second appendix.

\section{Planning}

To establish our schedule, we used GanttProject, which proved very helpful for planning the different project steps and distributing tasks.

To distribute tasks efficiently, we first identified what each member preferred not to do. Afterward, we assigned specific domains of activity based on everyone's preferences:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Maxime Fabre} focused on the robot's vision system for obstacle detection and localization.
    \item \textbf{Rayan Guedouir} managed the power system, ensuring monitoring of the energy stored inside the robot.
    \item \textbf{Thibaut Martin} worked on the mechanical structure and movement systems.
    \item \textbf{Mickaël Borges} focused on the actuators.
\end{itemize}
